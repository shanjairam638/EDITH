01 — THE PROBLEM

Most repair guidance tells you what to do.

But beginners often don't know why they are doing it, what they should observe, or what the result means.

01

Too Much Information

Long repair guides can overwhelm someone who has little technical experience.

02

No Learning Loop

Traditional guides rarely ask what happened after the user performs a step.

03

Same Advice for Everyone

A beginner and an experienced technician should not receive the same level of explanation.

02 — THE SOLUTION

EDITH turns repair into guided learning.

Instead of dumping instructions on the user, EDITH continuously teaches, asks, observes and adapts.

SAFETY FIRST

EDITH teaches responsibly.

The system is designed around safe, appropriate troubleshooting. If a situation could involve electrical hazards, damaged batteries, exposed components or other unsafe conditions, EDITH should stop procedural guidance and recommend qualified help.
